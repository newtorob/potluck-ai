# Product planning record — 18 March 2026
The billing team reviewed requests from six account administrators who reconcile appointments against monthly invoices. Several wanted to receive an export without signing in on the first working day of each month. Others wanted the same rows to flow into their existing bookkeeping system. The team grouped these requests separately because delivery by email requires permissions and retention decisions, while a programmatic interface requires an authentication design and public documentation. The team agreed that a missing feature should not be described as a customer-specific configuration issue. Support may share the current manual workflow and record the customer's desired delivery format, but should not promise an implementation date. No customer request in this review altered the current one-account scope, and no decision was made to introduce cross-account consolidation. The roadmap is a statement of intent and remains subject to engineering review, security review, and competing priorities. It is not evidence that a customer can use the feature today.

The team plans to pilot scheduled email delivery with a small group in the third quarter of 2026. An export API is only under consideration; it is not approved or scheduled.
