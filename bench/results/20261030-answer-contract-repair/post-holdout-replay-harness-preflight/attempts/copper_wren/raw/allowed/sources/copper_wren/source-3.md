# Follow-up support report — March 2026 cohort
The report includes cases submitted from March 1 through March 31 and resolved by the reporting cutoff. Operations used the same version 3 definition and event-log extraction as for the January baseline. A new routing queue was introduced between the periods, but the comparison alone does not establish that the queue caused the observed change. Changes in the mix of customer questions, staffing, or the number of cases still open at cutoff may also influence the published median. The team maintained a separate table of open cases and did not assign those cases an artificial resolution duration. For this report, standard priority is the priority recorded at submission, so an agent's later change to a ticket's label does not retrospectively put it in or out of the cohort. Customers' names were removed from the aggregate report before the results were shared with the product team. Each report describes submitted cases from its own month, rather than the exact same tickets followed across both months. March is the after period in the improvement review.

The March cohort's median business time to first resolution was 3.75 business days. The report compares this median with the January median of 5 business days, using January as the baseline.
