# Supplier incident playbook — section 2: assessment
After intake, the quality lead checks the affected lot against the receiving records and identifies whether other shipments may contain the same material. The lead may request lab measurements or inspection certificates from the supplier. Missing certificates are logged as gaps rather than treated as proof that a part failed. When a lot cannot be identified, the lead records the uncertainty and asks the warehouse to preserve the relevant delivery records. The assessment distinguishes items still in Harbor Rivet custody from items already delivered to a project site; these groups need different follow-up. Quality records its reasoning and any unresolved evidence in the same restricted incident register. A hold stays in place during assessment unless an authorized quality decision changes it. The supplier can supply evidence, but its proposed interpretation does not by itself release material. The lead also checks whether a similar report exists for a different supplier and keeps unrelated reports separate unless a real common cause is established.

The quality lead decides the affected scope and documents the evidence and uncertainties. For any material already delivered, the quality lead hands the scoped list to the field safety manager to coordinate site action. The decision about a customer site is not delegated to the intake coordinator.
