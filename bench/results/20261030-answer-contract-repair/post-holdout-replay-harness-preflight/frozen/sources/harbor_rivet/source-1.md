# Supplier incident playbook — section 1: intake
Harbor Rivet buys fabricated metal fittings for commercial building projects. An incident is any report that a delivered fitting may create a safety risk, including a suspected material mix-up, an incomplete inspection record, or a physical failure in a field installation. The person receiving the report records the supplier, shipment identifiers, site, and available photographs in the incident register. Reports may come from a project site, a supplier, the quality lab, or a warehouse worker. A report does not need a confirmed failure before it enters the register. The intake team avoids asking a field worker to remove a fitted part until site safety has assessed whether it is safe to do so. If there is a risk of immediate injury, the site follows its normal emergency procedure in parallel. The register should capture the first notification time and any gaps in shipment identification so that quality can request the missing information later. Customer names are recorded in the restricted register, not in the general supplier channel.

The intake coordinator opens the incident record and places the affected shipment on hold. The coordinator then hands the record and available evidence to the quality lead for assessment. A hold prevents further release of that shipment; it does not automatically recall items already installed.
