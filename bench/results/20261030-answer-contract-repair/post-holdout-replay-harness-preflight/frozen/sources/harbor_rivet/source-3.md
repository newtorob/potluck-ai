# Supplier incident playbook — section 3: action and closure
The field safety manager contacts the affected project sites identified by quality and agrees safe inspection or containment actions with the responsible site contacts. For products still in the warehouse, quality coordinates with warehouse operations on segregation and any further testing. Site actions should record who was contacted, the date of contact, and whether a site has acknowledged the proposed action. A site may need additional time to obtain access or a specialist inspection, so contact alone is not the same as completed containment. The manager flags unresponsive sites to the incident review meeting rather than assuming that the work is complete. Supporting material is retained in the restricted register and any corrections to a lot identifier are explained in the record. Supplier response documents are attached with their date, and any open technical question remains visible to the quality lead. The company does not require removal of every delivered part when assessment supports a narrower response.

The field safety manager records site acknowledgments and completed containment, then hands the action record back to the quality lead. The quality lead closes the incident only after verifying the actions and documenting any remaining follow-up owner. Closing the register entry is an administrative decision and does not erase the evidence.
