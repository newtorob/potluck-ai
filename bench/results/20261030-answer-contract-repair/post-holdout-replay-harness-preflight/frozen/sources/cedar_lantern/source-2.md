# Billing export boundaries — support handbook, 13 March 2026
The downloadable file is available to accounts on all paid plans. The permission check follows the current account administrator role, not the role of the person who originally created the account. Removing an administrator immediately removes their access to future downloads. Customer support can explain what each column means, but support staff cannot generate a customer export from the support console. For privacy, customer success should avoid asking users to email a full file; a redacted example of an unexpected row is usually enough to investigate a discrepancy. Amounts are expressed in the currency used on the invoice for the account. The file has no conversion column, and any comparison between accounts in different currencies needs to happen outside the product. This matters for a distributor with several independently billed accounts, because each account generates its own report and has its own invoice. A user can reconcile those reports externally, but cannot select multiple accounts in the current export screen.

The export does not run on a schedule or send files by email. It has no API and cannot export more than one account at a time. These are product limits as of this note, not temporary incidents or permission errors. The team has no commitment to provide a combined multi-account report.
