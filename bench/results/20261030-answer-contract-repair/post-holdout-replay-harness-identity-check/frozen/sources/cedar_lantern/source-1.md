# Customer operations note — 12 March 2026
Cedar Lantern sells a warehouse scheduling service to distributors. Its billing export is a customer-controlled report rather than an accounting integration. Account administrators open Settings, select a month, and download a CSV showing completed appointments and the associated charge for each location. The export includes the local appointment date, warehouse identifier, and charge amount. The report is generated when the administrator requests it, so two downloads may differ if a completed appointment was later corrected by the operations team. The CSV is intended to help finance teams reconcile a monthly invoice; it is not a tax document and does not itself pay or modify an invoice. Customers have asked for richer grouping, but the current interface has only the month selector and the location identifier in each row. Administrators can download prior closed months as well as the current month. The current month may be incomplete until the month ends. For internal troubleshooting, support asks the administrator for the export month and account name rather than a copy of the entire file. The support team can compare the selected period with invoice line items and explain timing differences without making changes to the customer account.
 
- The current export is a manually downloaded CSV for one selected month.
- Only account administrators can generate it.
- A month may contain corrections made after a prior download.
