# Service credit eligibility

Lumen offers a service credit when a paid customer's production workspace is unavailable for more than four consecutive hours because of a Lumen service incident. Planned maintenance announced at least 48 hours ahead and outages caused solely by the customer's configuration are excluded. The credit equals ten percent of that workspace's monthly subscription charge.

The rule applies to the affected workspace, not every workspace owned by the customer. A customer must request a credit within thirty days of the incident ending.
