# Harbor export scheduling

Harbor workspace administrators can schedule a recurring CSV export of completed orders from the reporting page. The scheduler is available to all paid workspaces as of the September release. Each schedule sends a download link to the administrator who created it; the link expires after seven days. Scheduling does not grant access to other reports, and the recipient still needs an active workspace account to download the file.

The team considers a schedule active once its first delivery has been queued. Workspace owners can pause or delete a schedule from the same page. Pausing keeps the configuration, while deleting it removes the configuration and its delivery history from the page.
