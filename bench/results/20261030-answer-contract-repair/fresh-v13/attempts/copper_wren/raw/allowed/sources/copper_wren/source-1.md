# Support measurement specification — version 3
Copper Wren provides workflow software for independent repair shops. The support operations team publishes a monthly duration measure to understand how long ordinary cases take to resolve. Each observation starts when a customer submits a new support case and ends at the first time that case is marked resolved by an agent. If a resolved case is later reopened, the initial resolution timestamp remains the endpoint of this measure. The team uses business time rather than elapsed clock time, counting eight working hours per business day and excluding nights and weekends. The median is calculated across case-level durations, not by taking the mean of daily medians. Cases are grouped by the month in which they were submitted, and the calculation is rerun after the follow-up period has ended so that late resolutions can be counted. Cases that remain unresolved at the cutoff are handled under a separate open-case report and do not enter the resolved-case median. This metric is intended to describe speed for this narrowly defined case population; it is not a measure of customer satisfaction or engineering delivery time.

The measurement covers standard-priority support cases submitted by paying repair shops. It excludes urgent cases, internal test tickets, and cases unresolved at the reporting cutoff. Business time excludes nights and weekends and counts eight working hours per business day.
