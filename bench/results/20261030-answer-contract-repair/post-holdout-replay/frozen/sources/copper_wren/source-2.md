# Baseline support report — January 2026 cohort
The report includes cases submitted from January 1 through January 31 and resolved by the reporting cutoff. The support team applied the version 3 metric definition consistently to this cohort before publishing it. Case submission times and resolution times came from the support system's event log, and the operations analyst checked the resulting duration distribution for duplicate records. Internal training exercises were tagged before extraction so they did not enter the cohort. The cohort was not restricted to a single day of the week or to a single support agent, and it covers standard-priority cases from paying repair shops. The published value is a median across the qualifying individual cases. It is not a target or a guaranteed maximum for an individual customer. The report includes a separate open-case count, because excluding unresolved cases means the median does not describe how long those outstanding cases will eventually take. The team cautions against comparing this figure to an elapsed-clock-time metric without conversion. January is the before period in the improvement review.

The January cohort's median business time to first resolution was 5 business days. This baseline uses the same case definition as the later March cohort.
