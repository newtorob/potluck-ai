# Supplier invoice intake

At Orchard, the accounts payable coordinator receives supplier invoices in the shared invoice mailbox. The coordinator checks that each invoice names a supplier and purchase order, then enters it in the ledger with status Received. Missing purchase order details are returned to the supplier for correction; the invoice does not enter the approval queue until corrected.

For invoices with complete intake details, the coordinator attaches the original invoice to the ledger record and routes the record to the budget owner named on the purchase order.
