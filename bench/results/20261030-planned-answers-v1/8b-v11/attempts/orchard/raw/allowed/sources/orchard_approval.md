# Budget approval

The budget owner compares the invoice amount with the purchase order and confirms that the goods or services were received. If both checks pass, the owner marks the invoice Approved in the ledger. A mismatch is sent back to accounts payable for investigation, rather than being approved automatically.

Approval is the budget owner's decision. Accounts payable maintains the record and communicates with the supplier during an investigation, but does not substitute its own approval for the owner's decision.
