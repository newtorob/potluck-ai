# Birch working note 3

The operations team stores monthly delivery summaries by calendar month. Use
the completed count, rather than the number of bookings, when comparing
periods.

The office keeps a separate log for day-to-day questions. Each entry contains the
date received and a brief description, followed by the initials of the person
who filed it. Entries are reviewed in batches, and outdated drafts stay labeled
as drafts until someone supplies a verified replacement.
