# Migration delivery note — 4 April 2026
Osprey Quay operates a booking service for independent ferry terminals. The delivery team is moving the reservation ledger from a legacy database to a managed database. The rollout plan separates the internal rehearsal, customer read-only window, and cutover because each event has different participants and rollback conditions. The rehearsal uses copied data and does not change customer bookings. The read-only window temporarily prevents new edits while the team checks the migrated ledger; customers can still view their existing bookings during that window. Cutover is the event at which the managed database becomes the live system of record. A rollback plan remains available if reconciliation fails, and the team will retain the old database until the post-cutover verification completes. The delivery team has told customer support to avoid describing the rehearsal as a customer launch. Individual terminals are not required to change their own software for this migration. The implementation work applies to all terminals served by the shared booking service, rather than just a single pilot terminal.

The delivery note schedules the internal rehearsal for 14 April and the live ledger cutover for 21 April 2026. These are separate events; the rehearsal does not switch production traffic.
